Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · App: LacakTrans (Android com.silsilara.lacaktrans and web)

This policy explains what data LacakTrans uses, why, and where it is stored. In short: no account. Favorites and settings stay on your device. Your location coordinates are read to find nearby stops and are never sent to any server, because LacakTrans has no server for user data. Usage statistics only include the names of the district (kecamatan), regency/city, and province you are in.

1. Location

If you allow it, LacakTrans reads your device location to show nearby stops and stations and to center the map on you.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Favorite stops and stations.
  • Settings: light/dark theme, language, and the mode filter on the map.
  • The latest schedule data downloaded from the LacakTrans site, so the app also works offline.
  • A random usage statistics code (see section 4), the last area names (see section 4), and a marker for postponed update notices.
  • On Android: a temporary Story card image when you share it; older images are deleted when you share again.

This data is never sent to a LacakTrans server.

3. Services contacted directly from your device

To show the map and vehicle positions, the app fetches data directly from its providers:

  • TransJakarta fleet feed: bus positions for the map area currently in view. TransJakarta's server receives requests per map area, not your GPS location.
  • OpenFreeMap: map images (tiles) for the area being viewed.
  • The LacakTrans site (Vercel): latest schedules, version info, disruption banners, and area boundary maps for the area around you.

Like any internet service, these providers can see the IP address of the device requesting data. Google Maps, WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and other apps are only opened when you tap a share or "Directions here" button, and are subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Usage statistics

Anonymous usage statistics, including the district (kecamatan), regency/city, and province when location permission is granted, are sent to an analytics service to prioritise development, without an account, name, or GPS coordinates.

5. Sharing data

LacakTrans does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and shows no ads. The service providers used to run LacakTrans only process data for that purpose: Vercel (site and schedule data), an analytics service (usage statistics), Google Drive (APK file), TransJakarta (bus positions), and OpenFreeMap (map). These providers may process data outside Indonesia.

6. Security

All app communication uses encrypted connections (HTTPS and WSS). There are no accounts or passwords to protect because LacakTrans does not use accounts.

7. Deleting data

  • Remove favorites one by one from the stop or station screen.
  • Delete all data on the device via Android Settings → Apps → LacakTrans → Storage → Clear data, by uninstalling the app, or by clearing site data in your browser for the web version.
  • Revoke location permission at any time in your phone or browser settings.

8. Children

LacakTrans does not ask for personal data and can be used without an account by anyone. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the latest version and its effective date will be updated on this page.

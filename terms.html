1. The service

LacakTrans is a free app for viewing public transport schedules and positions in Jakarta and its surroundings on one map, without an account. Features may change, be added, or be discontinued at any time.

2. Data sources and accuracy

  • TransJakarta bus positions come from TransJakarta's public fleet feed and may be delayed, missing, or wrong.
  • KRL, MRT, LRT Jabodebek, and LRT Jakarta positions are estimated from schedules, not from train GPS, and may differ from reality, especially during disruptions or schedule changes.
  • Schedules, routes, and coordinates come from open sources (TransJakarta GTFS, the community Comuline API, OpenStreetMap) and may not reflect the latest situation.
  • Disruption banners are recorded manually from operators' official accounts and may be updated late.

For official information, always check each operator's official channels.
